%% =============================================================================
%%  fig_filter.tex -- the snoop filter's internal mechanism, standalone.
%%
%%  Build:  pdflatex fig_filter.tex   ->  fig_filter.pdf
%%
%%  Drawn in the terminology of the paper's Section V, so that every block and
%%  signal here is defined in the text:
%%    update port from each DCU: "tag + valid update" (the signals that write
%%        its Status-RAM and Tag-RAM), and "fill in flight + address"
%%    the INV REQ selected by the INV REQ arbiter: issued by PE i, address of w
%%        = set index . tag
%%    per (PE, way): Tag-RAM mirror (LUTRAM, read at the set index of w),
%%        valid bit (flip-flop), comparator "stored tag = tag of w", AND with
%%        the valid bit
%%    per PE: OR over the two ways -> "holds the line"; fill comparator ->
%%        "is fetching the line"; OR -> "holds or is fetching"
%%    exclude the requesting PE i -> sharer set S(w);  NOR -> S(w) = empty ->
%%        suppress the broadcast, else broadcast and grant by condition (1)
%%
%%  Routing rule, so that no two lines cross: the "address of w" is one bus
%%  that threads left to right through every block that reads it (Tag-RAM
%%  mirror, valid bit, fill comparator); those blocks take their DCU inputs
%%  from above, the tag comparator hangs below the bus on a tap (junction
%%  dot), and every result flows downwards. Gate inputs are reached by
%%  orthogonal jogs at different heights, the left source always feeding the
%%  left input, so the jogs never cross. Gates are the US shapes rotated so
%%  their inputs face up: after rotate=-90, "input 2" is the LEFT input and
%%  "input 1" the right one (input 4 leftmost on the 4-input NOR).
%%  PE 0 is drawn in full; PE 1-3 are identical and drawn collapsed.
%% =============================================================================
